Model and comparison

For an accepted plan, P*τ|k denotes the optimised power for target interval τ. The applied command is Pactk = P*k|k; later actions are provisional. This replay contains accepted plans. The notebook also supplies backup handling for rejected plans or unavailable readings.

Lendτ and Uendτ constrain the temperature at boundary τ+1, the end of interval τ. Prices, comfort schedules and the issued weather forecasts are available when the plan is made.

The core objective combines predicted electricity cost and lower-comfort slack at £25/(°C h), with hard upper comfort and 0–5 kW heating limits. The richer air/fabric reference produces the realised temperatures. Each run uses the same initial conditions, weather, schedules and noise sequence.

For this supplied day, rolling and shrinking horizons give essentially the same realised actions and metrics. Compare their plan lengths and endpoints. The replay control moves through saved controller solves; it preserves the original forecasts and measurements.
